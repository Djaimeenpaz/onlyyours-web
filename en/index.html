FAQ
What people usually ask

    
Is the song really original?
Yes. The lyrics are written from your story and the music is produced for that order. We never reuse lyrics or songs between clients.

    
How is the music made?
We combine human writing and creative direction with AI music-production tools. A person reviews every lyric and every mix before delivery. That's how we can be fast and affordable without sounding like a template.

    
Can I ask for changes?
Yes. Every package includes rounds of lyric changes. We want the song to say exactly what you mean.

    
How long does it take?
Song, 48 h from receiving payment and story; Song + Video, 72 h; Event/Business, priority delivery based on the event date; Legacy Song, 7 days or sooner if needed. If it's urgent, tell us.

    
What about a Legacy Song if I don't want anyone to hear it yet?
We deliver it only to the person you name, with your instructions on when to open it, and we never publish it or use it as an example. Absolute discretion.

    
Can I use it on social media, at the wedding or at an event?
Yes, for personal use and at the event it was ordered for. For commercial or advertising use, ask us.

    
How do I pay?
By credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through a secure gateway; no PayPal account needed, although you can use one. We don't store payment data.
